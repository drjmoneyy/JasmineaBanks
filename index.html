Jasmine Banks

Ph.D. Candidate in Psychology, Certificate in Digital Studies
University of Michigan, Ann Arbor

My research examines online behaviors at the intersection of race, identity, and culture, utilizing diverse methodologies. My research agenda explores individual and collective world-building practices within personal, social, and intimate spheres, with particular attention to how these practices are mediated by technology.
