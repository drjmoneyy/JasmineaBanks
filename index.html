Jasmine Banks

6th-Year Ph.D. Candidate in Psychology and Digital Studies
University of Michigan, Ann Arbor

I am a multidisciplinary researcher interested in the world-building practices of humans, especially as they intersect with technology. My research spans both academic and applied contexts, and also explores public scholarship and artistic practice as ways of generating and sharing knowledge. I draw on qualitative, mixed-methods, and experimental methods in my work. I also have experience conducting community-based research, curriculum development, and program evaluation, working with homeless shelters, schools, and other programs.
