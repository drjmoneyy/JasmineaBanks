Public engagement

Speaking & Media Opportunities

I am available for public speaking, guest lectures, and media appearances. I value connecting with audiences to share my research and to start substantive conversations about the intersections of psychology, digital culture, and identity.

What I Can Speak About

Topics I am prepared to bring to your audience.

Psychology of Online Behaviors

How digital spaces shape identity and community.

Online Racism

Exploring the impact of racial discrimination in digital spaces.

Research Methods and Ethics

Tips for conducting ethical and meaningful social media research, especially qualitative studies.

Black Digital Culture

From memes and humor to community building, exploring how Black culture thrives online.

Racism, Sexism, and Bias in Online Dating

Understanding the challenges and historical influences behind bias in dating apps.

Historical Context in Black Digital Experiences

How colonialism, racism, and sexism shape online interactions.

Intersectionality and Online Identity

The interplay of race, gender, and identity in digital self-expression.

Digital Strategies for Inclusion

Using technology to empower and include marginalized voices.

Community-Based Research

Why collaboration with communities matters in research.

Social Media Presence as an Academic

How to build an authentic and impactful online presence.

Let's Work Together

If any of these topics resonate with your audience, I would welcome the opportunity to speak at your event, join a panel, or collaborate on a workshop. Whether the setting is a lecture series, a conference, or a casual talk, I bring research-grounded perspectives and aim to spark substantive conversation.
